# Product Requirements

## Product

XingShe is an Android, local-first tool for photography enthusiasts to discover
shooting spots and record photography trips.

## MVP outcomes

- Sign in with an email verification code.
- Browse, search, view, and favorite shooting spots.
- Start, pause, resume, and finish a locally recorded trip.
- Attach captured or imported photos without uploading originals.
- Review trip routes and statistics and generate a privacy-conscious share image.

## Out of scope

Social feeds, comments, payments, cloud track synchronization, online photo
storage, iOS, a web admin console, and production deployment are not part of the
MVP.
